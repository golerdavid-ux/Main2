Why I Write This

You don't need a course. You need to see what's possible.

There is a lot of noise around AI right now. Every week brings a new tool, a new model, a new reason to panic or get excited. Most of it is not useful to you.

I started this memo because I believe the best way to understand AI is to use it. Not to take a certification. Not to watch a 40-minute YouTube breakdown. Just sit down, try something, and see what happens.

AI tools today are intuitive enough that they don't require formal training. They're like calculators. You don't need a course to use a calculator. You just need to know it exists and what it can do.

The people who will do best with AI are the ones who understand both what it can do and where it falls short. This memo is my attempt to help you get there, one week at a time.
