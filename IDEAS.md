# Future App Ideas

## Scheduling App (Personal + Business)

**Status:** Idea - not yet started

**Concept:**
A scheduling app that connects to both personal and business calendars and can schedule meetings for either one.

**Key Features:**
- Dual calendar integration (personal + business)
- Schedule meetings on either calendar
- Daily task tracking / routine reminders:
  - Taking pills
  - Washing face
  - Brushing teeth
  - Other daily self-care routines
- User-facing dashboard for managing all of the above
